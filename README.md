I started my journey in design more than 10 years ago.

Today, I work at Monks creating digital experiences across multiple formats — from HTML5 Ads and Motion Graphics to automation systems that optimize creative workflows.

Over time, curiosity evolved into something deeper:

understanding how systems work.

That curiosity pushed me beyond design and into development, automation and tooling.

I enjoy building things that are:
- useful
- fast
- organized
- scalable
- simple

I especially enjoy solving problems that nobody wants to touch.
